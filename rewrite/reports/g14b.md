Per chunk: x = a, or bf16(silu(a) * b) with silu = a / (1 + exp2(a * -1.442695)) (IEEE div); `x*su` (fused first butterfly stage with fma on the 4th element),

## Bound
The rotated rows sit in LDS at row*5120 B + chunk*256 B (T=2 `ds_load_b128 ... offset:5120` @960F48, T=4 5120/10240/15360 @965194-@965240; g22b gl<2> agrees),
so at most 20 chunks per row fit; the vendor code has no check, the host must keep nch <= 20. The rewrite sizes R for 20 chunks and traps above that.
Specs at the limit: K = 2560 with cpb = 20 (`_s20w`, T = 2, 4, one split) and cpb = 16 (`_s16x`, two splits of 16 + 4); T = 1 `_s16w` K = 2048, cpb = 16 (T = 1 base 9728..13824 gives 16 chunks).
