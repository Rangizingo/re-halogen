## dnc_ut5 (24/24): `dnc_ut5.hip`
WMMA form of ut4 (f16 operands x16, results x2^-8). Kernel names `dnc_ut5_n<norm>_r<rs>_a<b3>_k<kl>`.
- norm (2nd bool): K = bf16 key columns of qkv times scale a8, zero for token >= n.
- rs 0: scalar forward substitution. rs 1: fully unrolled schedule of the same chain (bitwise equal to rs 0). rs 2: no substitution. rs 3: blocked solve (diagonal 16-blocks, then B = chain over earlier blocks, C = apply diagonal block inverse); different rounding from rs 0, reproduced.
- b3 (ATT): extra Q16.K16 WMMA tiles -> a10 f16(16*S) and a11 f32, masked col<=row, upper tiles written as zero.
- b4 (burst): schedule only. b5 (KL, needs b4): K operand is the dequantised f16 K16 copy.
Specs: `dnc_ut5_{base,norm}[_rs1|_rs2|_rs3|_rs3_kl]`, `att[_norm][_rs1|_kl]`, `*_edge`.
